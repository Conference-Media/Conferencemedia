How much does it cost?

Coverage starts at $1,750. Every conference is different, so we quote each one. Tell us your date and budget, and we'll send a fixed quote that fits it.

How big is your crew?

It depends on your event. A one-day, single-room conference usually needs two people. Larger events get up to six, including a dedicated editor on site.

Do you travel outside Toronto?

Yes. We cover events across Canada and have recently worked in Montreal. Travel costs are included in your quote.

Can you cover an event in French?

Yes. Our founder speaks French and conducts and edits French interviews himself.

Do you work with the venue's AV team?

Yes. We coordinate with your venue and AV provider before the event so we can record clean audio from the stage.

Can our sponsors get their own photos?

Yes. We photograph every booth and sort the gallery by sponsor, so you can send each partner their own set.
